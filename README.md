# Kahani 🌍

> Don't just visit. Leave with a story.

## About

Kahani is a travel experience website built around one simple idea:

**Don't just book a destination. Book a local life.**

The goal is to help travellers discover experiences based on the kind of story they want to live — not just the destination they want to visit.

## The Problem

When we travel, we often follow the usual itineraries and popular places.

Sometimes, we return home and realize we missed the experiences that could have made the trip truly memorable.

Kahani starts with a simple question:

**What kind of experience do you want to live?**
