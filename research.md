## What I Found

### 1. Airbnb Experiences

What I found:

- Airbnb has three main sections: Homes, Experiences and Services.
- Experiences include activities such as food, culture, adventure, arts, wellness and more.
- Users can search based on destination and dates and use available filters.
- Airbnb connects travellers with local hosts who provide experiences.
- Airbnb also suggests stays and other options when searching.
- If something is unavailable, Airbnb can provide alternative options.

What Airbnb does well:

- Large number of listings
- Trusted booking platform
- Local experience hosts
- Good search and filtering
- Reviews and ratings
- Pricing and availability information
- Suggestions and alternatives

What I think is missing:

- The experience discovery still starts mainly from the destination and available listings.
- It feels more like a booking marketplace than a travel companion.
- Smaller, informal/local experiences may not easily fit into the platform's host requirements.
- A traveller looking for a specific type of local life may have to search through listings instead of describing the kind of experience they want.

## Gap / Opportunity for Kahani 

Kahani can focus on the traveller first.

Instead of:
"Where are you going and what can you book?"

Kahani can ask:
"What kind of experience do you want to live?"

##Airbnb:
Destination → Available experiences → Book

##Kahani:
Traveller → What kind of life do you want? → Discover your story

It can include both bookable experiences and smaller local recommendations,
making Kahani feel more like a travel companion than a marketplace.
